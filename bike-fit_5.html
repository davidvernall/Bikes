Step 3 of 6
Body measurements
ℹ️ Measure in bare feet, standing against a wall. Use centimetres. Fitted clothing gives the most accurate results.
Inseam (cm)
Book between your legs, as high as comfortable. Measure floor to book spine.
Enter your inseam (50–120 cm).
Height (cm)
Stand straight, back against the wall, heels flat.
Enter your height (120–220 cm).
Torso length (cm)
Sit upright on a flat surface. Measure from the seat to the top of your shoulder.
Enter your torso length (30–90 cm).
Arm length (cm)
Shoulder to wrist bone, elbow bent 15–20°.
Enter your arm length (40–90 cm).
Shoulder width (cm)
Across the bony points on top of each shoulder.
Enter shoulder width (25–65 cm).
